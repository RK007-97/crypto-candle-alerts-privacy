Privacy Policy for Crypto Candle Alerts

Effective date: 13 October 2026

Crypto Candle Alerts ("the app") is an Android app that watches public cryptocurrency market prices and sounds an alarm when a condition you set is met. It is provided by Rakesh Shingane ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with information.

Summary

The app has no accounts and no backend server of mine. I do not collect, store, sell or share your personal information.

Information stored on your device

The alerts you create (symbol, timeframe, condition, price or indicator values) and your settings are stored only on your phone. I never receive them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Network requests

To check prices, the app sends requests directly from your phone to the public market-data service of Delta Exchange India (api.india.delta.exchange). Each request contains the market symbol and timeframe you chose. As with any internet request, the service can see your device's IP address. These requests are governed by Delta Exchange's own terms and privacy policy. The app does not send your name, email, location, contacts or any account credentials. The app never asks for exchange logins or API keys and never places trades.

Subscriptions and payments

The optional premium subscription is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play, and I do not see or store your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your subscription is active.

Advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Not financial advice

Alerts are informational only and are not financial advice. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by Delta Exchange.
